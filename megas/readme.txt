=== Megas Greek Coffee ===
Requires at least: 6.4
Tested up to: 6.8
Requires PHP: 8.0
Version: 1.0.16
License: GPL-2.0-or-later

Custom brand theme for Megas Greek Coffee. Configure homepage content under Appearance > Customize > Megas. Local fonts and brand artwork included. Contact form uses WordPress mail; configure a real recipient and verify delivery on your hosting. Polylang support is optional and requires language/page setup.
